02Featured system

For home-care agencies

The Home Care Inquiry System

When a family starts looking for care, they're often reaching out to several agencies at once. The agency that responds first — clearly and warmly — has the best chance of earning their trust.

The Home Care Inquiry System connects your website, inquiry form, automatic acknowledgments, and owner notifications into one workflow, configured around how your agency already operates.

  1. Family 01

    Visits your website

    A prospective client finds a clear, trustworthy agency website.

  2. Family 02

    Submits an inquiry

    A simple form captures who they are and the care they're looking for.

  3. Automatic 03

    Receives an acknowledgment

    They get an immediate confirmation that their inquiry arrived.

  4. Automatic 04

    You're notified

    The agency owner receives the lead details right away.

  5. Your team 05

    You follow up

    Your team reaches out while the family's interest is still high.

Every agency is different. We'll map your current intake process first, then build the system around it and the tools you already use.
