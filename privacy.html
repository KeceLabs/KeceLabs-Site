Privacy Policy

This document is a good-faith summary of how KeceLabs operates. It is provided for transparency and may be updated as our services and practices change. It is not legal advice.

01Information we collect

We collect information you choose to give us, primarily when you submit the contact form on this website. This may include:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (optional, unless you ask to receive text messages)
  • Your business name (optional)
  • The service you're interested in and the message you submit
  • Your SMS consent choice, if you check the optional SMS box

We also receive information when you email us directly or communicate with us during a project. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically process basic technical information — such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested — in server logs to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

02How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and schedule a call.
  • Understand your needs and prepare recommendations or proposals.
  • Provide, manage, and support services you engage us for.
  • Send text messages about your inquiry and related services, only if you have given SMS consent.
  • Keep records of our communications and agreements.
  • Protect our website and business from spam, fraud, and misuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

03SMS messaging and consent

The SMS consent checkbox on our contact form is optional, unchecked by default, and not required to submit the form or to purchase any service. If you check it, you authorize KeceLabs to send text messages related to your inquiry and our services to the phone number you provide.

  • Message frequency varies based on your inquiry and our conversation.
  • Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.
  • Reply STOP to any message to opt out. You may receive one final message confirming that you've been unsubscribed.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or email hello@kecelabs.com.

Mobile information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. SMS opt-in data and consent records are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties, except with the service providers that help us deliver messages on our behalf, or where required by law.

04How information is stored and protected

Contact form submissions are processed and stored by our website hosting and form-processing provider and delivered to KeceLabs by email. We may also keep relevant details in our email account and in the business tools we use to manage client communications.

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, such as limiting access to people who need it, using reputable service providers, and using accounts protected by strong passwords and, where available, two-factor authentication. The website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

No method of transmitting or storing information online is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

05Third-party service providers

We rely on trusted third-party providers to run our website and communicate with you. These providers process information on our behalf and under their own privacy policies. Currently they include:

  • Netlify — website hosting and contact form processing.
  • Google Fonts — delivers the typefaces used on this site. Your browser requests font files from Google, which receives your IP address as part of that request.
  • Email and messaging providers — used to send and receive emails and, if you have consented, text messages.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights, or as part of a business transfer such as a merger or sale.

When we build systems for our clients, the client's own customer data is handled according to the client agreement and the client's own privacy policy.

06Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics tools. We do not set cookies to track you across websites.

The interactive Lead Response System demo on our homepage is a simulation that runs entirely in your browser. It has no input fields, does not collect any information, and does not send calls, texts, or emails.

Our hosting provider may use strictly necessary technical mechanisms to deliver the site securely. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy before doing so.

07Data retention

We keep inquiry information for as long as it's reasonably needed to respond to you, provide services, maintain business records, and meet legal, tax, or accounting obligations. Inquiries that don't lead to a project are periodically reviewed and deleted when no longer needed.

If you opt out of SMS, we keep a record of your opt-out so that we continue to honor it.

08Your choices and requests

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete your information, subject to records we must keep by law.
  • Stop sending you text messages or emails.

To make a request, email hello@kecelabs.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

09Children’s privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be made clear on this page.

11Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact KeceLabs at hello@kecelabs.com.
